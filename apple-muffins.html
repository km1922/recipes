Apple Oatmeal Cinnamon Cottage Cheese Muffins

Yield: 24 muffins

Ingredients

  • 3 cups old-fashioned rolled oats
  • 2 cups whole wheat flour
  • 4 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 cups low-fat cottage cheese
  • 1 cup unsweetened applesauce
  • ⅔ cup pure maple syrup
  • 4 large eggs
  • 4 tbsp melted coconut oil or butter
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 3 cups sweet apples, finely diced
  • 1 cup dark or sugar-free chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Line two 12-cup muffin tins with paper liners.
  2. In a blender, process cottage cheese, applesauce, maple syrup, eggs, melted coconut oil, and vanilla extract until completely smooth.
  3. In a large bowl, whisk together oats, flour, cinnamon, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
  4. Fold wet mixture into dry ingredients, then gently stir in diced apples and chocolate chips.
  5. Divide batter into 24 muffin cups and bake for 20 to 24 minutes until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
  6. Cool in pan for 10 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack.
