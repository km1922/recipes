Hearty Pumpkin Maple Muffins

Yield: 24 muffins

Ingredients

  • 2 cups old-fashioned rolled oats
  • 2 cups whole wheat flour (or all-purpose flour)
  • 4 tsp baking powder
  • 2 tsp baking soda
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 2 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 tsp ground nutmeg
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 cups pumpkin puree
  • 1½ cups low-fat cottage cheese
  • 1⅓ cups pure maple syrup
  • 4 large eggs
  • 6 tbsp melted butter or coconut oil
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 cup dark chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Line two 12-cup muffin tins with paper liners or spray with non-stick cooking spray.
  2. In a blender or food processor, combine pumpkin puree, cottage cheese, maple syrup, eggs, melted butter (or coconut oil), and vanilla extract. Blend on high speed until completely smooth and creamy.
  3. In a large bowl, whisk together rolled oats, whole wheat flour, baking powder, baking soda, cinnamon, ginger, nutmeg, and salt.
  4. Pour the blended wet mixture into the dry ingredients and stir gently until just combined. Fold in dark chocolate chips.
  5. Divide batter evenly across the 24 muffin cups. Bake for 22–25 minutes until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
  6. Cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.
